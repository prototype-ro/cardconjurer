# Theme Bible — the Vast Accord (Kaalia of the Vast Commander deck)

Working lore for the user's real Kaalia of the Vast deck. Built live, card by card — this file is the durable record of decisions made in conversation so future prompts don't re-derive or contradict them. Most cards in this deck keep their real MTG name/mechanic; only specific cards get full "nickname" treatment (invented name, original design) per Section 2.

## 1. Cosmology

Same structural shape as a Tolkien-style legendarium — a supreme maker, lesser spirits under it, one spirit that rebels and corrupts others — but every proper noun is original, never Tolkien's own.

- **Lathaar** — the supreme maker; also the name for its unfallen, still-original spirits (near-divine, elegant, individual).
- **Maelgrith** — the one who rebelled against Lathaar and corrupts others; the corruptor. Corruption by Maelgrith works by **erasure/subtraction** (dissolving selfhood, fraying form into absence), not by monstrous addition — that distinguishes fully-submitted design language from mid-tier corruption.
- **Aelathir** — a corrupted-but-still-a-person spirit; deep-corrupted with retained agency and identity (most named individual characters land here).
- **Aelgrath** — the faction name for corrupted spirits generally, spanning the whole corruption spectrum (Section 2). Not a card-type synonym for "demon" — Aelgrath cards can be any real card type/mechanic reframed into this lore.

## 2. Corruption spectrum (divine → dissolved)

Use this to place any named/nicknamed Aelgrath-faction card before designing it.

1. **Lathaar-tier** — near-original, still elegant/individual, barely touched. (Untested in art as of this writing.)
2. **Mid-transformation** — visibly split: body literally divided between an intact original-self half and an already-corrupted half. Corruption here still reads as addition/mutation (fused bone, cracked stone growing through), not erasure.
3. **Aelathir-tier** — deep-corrupted but still a person: has agency, motive, can bargain/scheme. Corruption is addition/mutation (fused bone, ash-cracked skin, extra horns, hoarder/collector traits) — never facelessness. Named characters here keep individual invented names. Roster so far: **Selgorath** (broker — still/settled pose, environmental surprising detail, rich 3-color contrast), **Vaelendris** (deceiver — one refined specific accessory, pale figure against varied environment), **Shilgengar** (kept real card name; ancient hoarder-collector; four horns total, two per side, no two matching; fallen-angel wings resting on the ground, not worn).
4. **Maelgrith-submitted** — fully dissolved into Maelgrith's will: no face, no individual name (title/function only, e.g. "the Measured Hand"), no retained agency. Corruption here is **erasure**, not addition: features fraying into smoke/void rather than growing extra material. First working design: a corrupted Exalted Sunborn-style silhouette (multi-wing hovering angel base) — feathers clearly defined near the body, wingtips dissolving into black smoke at the edges only; smooth featureless head with a single narrow point of violet light; robe patterned with small ragged tears showing nothing behind them, not stars.

**Design rule for this tier specifically:** "faceless" must never mean "shapeless." An earlier attempt (loose smoke/vapor form) was rejected as too formless. The fix is hard, countable, named geometry or anatomy (defined wing feathers, a named silhouette shape, exact finger/claw counts) with erasure happening only at the edges/extremities — not a diffuse blob. Materials for this tier should still read as organic/corrupted-body, never as armor plating or a mechanical/robot design (a plated-mecha draft was rejected as "too much out of the lore").

**Composition note:** Maelgrith-submitted characters have no interiority to play against a human reactor — an added bystander figure reads as an unwanted extra subject. Get the actor+reactor structure (art-template Rule 10) from the *stolen/affected thing itself* reacting (e.g. a half-formed stolen creature straining against the grip) and from environmental depth (faint silhouettes of past victims), not from a second person on-frame.

## 3. Signature color

**Violet is Maelgrith's/the void's signature color** — locked in as of the Lord of the Void redesign. Use violet for any tear/rift/void-adjacent light source or glow tied to Maelgrith's corruption or the void itself, across any corruption tier. Keep this distinct from whatever signature color Lathaar's own unfallen tier eventually gets (not yet established — flag if a Lathaar-tier card is designed before this is decided).

## 4. Named characters (nickname cards)

- **Selgorath** — Aelathir-tier broker. (Real card TBD/unrecorded in this file — see conversation history.)
- **Vaelendris** — Aelathir-tier deceiver. Real card: Bloodletter of Aclazotz (real name/mechanic/flavor ignored per user instruction, invented fresh).
- **Shilgengar** — kept real MTG name, Aelathir-tier design (four asymmetric horns, ground-resting wings).
- **Drana and Linvala** — kept real MTG names, first-draft design only, unconfirmed.
- **Lord of the Void** (real card: Ravnica Remastered, {4}{B}{B}{B}, 7/7 Flying Demon, "combat damage → exile top 7, put a creature from among them onto the battlefield under your control") — placed at **Maelgrith-submitted** tier per Section 2's worked design above. Mechanic reframed as reaching into an opponent's still-forming future and stealing the champion before it finishes arriving; faint past-victim silhouettes in the tear visualize the mechanic's repeatability. Name: kept the real card name, "Lord of the Void" — fits the tier's no-personal-identity rule as a title rather than a name.
  - **Confirmed design (accepted image):** two separate wing pairs (larger upper pair from the shoulders, smaller lower pair from the hip line, visible gap between them, never merging into one silhouette); wing feathers a lighter charcoal-grey than the matte-black robe so the two materials stay visually distinct; wingtips fray to black smoke at the tips only, solid near the body. Head is a crowned-helm silhouette in the same void-black material — tall jagged gothic-crown spires, faceless, no eyes/mouth — with hair streaming from beneath it made of the same void material but colored deep starlit navy-blue, faint pinprick starlight confined to the hair strands only (nowhere else in the image, to keep the "erasure not addition" rule intact elsewhere). A single thin violet seam glows between two crown spires. Right arm reaches into the violet tear; left arm hangs relaxed at its side (needed as an explicit clause — omitting it caused the generator to drop the second arm entirely). Background: dark navy-black storm sky with lightning, explicitly never flat/matte black and never washed-out/bright — needs both anchors stated together or MJ drifts to one extreme.
  - **Working note:** stacking two design changes at once (wing-material fix + crown/hair redesign) risked compounding failures per Rule 10's detail-budget principle; isolating fixes one at a time is safer when multiple things are wrong simultaneously, even though in this case the combined version worked out.

- **Rune-Scarred Demon** (real card: Foundations, {5}{B}{B}, 6/6 Flying Demon, "enters → search library for any card, put into hand") — Aelathir-tier, new archetype: **certainty**, not broker/deceiver/hoarder. Stands perfectly still amid a burning battlefield, completely unbothered, because in his mind the outcome was never in question — same stillness-amid-chaos structure as Selgorath but a different emotional register (unbothered certainty, not patient brokering). Confirmed design: deep near-black skin with an oil-slick sheen; smoke/shadow wings (barely-solid, drifting rather than spread); one eye open with mild polite curiosity, the other closed with an old rune across the eyelid that cycles through a full rainbow spectrum (ties directly to the mechanic — he can retrieve a card of any color); holds an irregular glowing violet crystal cluster loosely, unhurried; a defeated enemy's crown/helm lies knocked loose near his feet as the environmental spoil-of-battle detail. No key, no paper-wings, no cracked-bone-plate vocabulary — those were rejected drafts, see working note below.
  - **Working note (design process):** two earlier full drafts were rejected as "not iconic enough" (a library/vault-of-shelves concept, then a body-horror chest-furnace-door concept) despite being individually well-executed — the actual problem was starting from the mechanic's literal action (searching, producing) rather than the character's personality, same lesson as Selgorath/Vaelendris's original success. Running a structured battery of specific multiple-choice questions (accessory, held object, accent color, skin material, detail placement, wing material, environment remnant, demeanor) to pin down a design only worked well after the personality/emotional register was decided first — running that question battery on a mechanic-first concept had already failed twice before switching approaches.

- **Vaelgorath, Sire of Poison** — Vaelendris's mirror/counterpart (real card: Rakdos, Patron of Chaos, {4}{B}{R}, 6/6 Flying/Trample Demon, "end step: target opponent may sacrifice two nonland permanents, or you draw two cards") — Aelathir-tier, explicitly designed as a close visual counterpart to Vaelendris rather than a distinct archetype: same body template (tall, slender, long straight silver-white hair, pointed ears, dark robe with a cascading line of small glowing violet beads, still/composed stance), mirrored to face the opposite direction. Distinguishing swaps: deep **muted** red-toned skin (explicitly not saturated/cartoon devil-red — that read as too on-the-nose in earlier drafts), a dark serpent coiled on one forearm in place of the raven, a **deep black gem that holds no glow** (an absence of light) in place of Vaelendris's green-blue glowing gem, and a fitted gold mask-crown with a handful of sharp points close-contoured to the skull (not a wide radial spike-burst/halo — that was an intermediate failure, corrected). Weapon and crown were both built from real-world reference images (an antique gold hilt with mother-of-pearl grip and hound's-head crossguard terminals; a gold spiked mask-crown) rather than pure invented text description. Environment intentionally reuses Vaelendris's own cool mist / warm rose-glow backdrop rather than staging the mechanic literally, per the "intentional mirror" framing.
  - **Working note (design process):** an earlier full attempt at this same real card (a bulky, muscular, hulking "prideful conqueror" demon descending into burning ruins) was abandoned entirely after repeated failures — reads as generic demon-lord, drifted to unwanted feminine/beefcake readings depending on phrasing, and a giant-scythe misfire from over-describing the blade as "curved." The approach that actually worked was completely different: start from an already-confirmed successful design (Vaelendris) and modify it as a deliberate close variant/counterpart via targeted questionnaire (relationship to Vaelendris, companion animal, environment, one signature accent), then swap in real object reference images (an actual antique sword hilt, an actual mask-crown illustration) for the weapon and headpiece instead of describing them from imagination. When an original design keeps failing after many attempts, pivoting to "modify a known-good design" plus real reference images is a strong fallback, not a lesser approach.

- **Sildarion, the Unbending Grace** (real card: Esper Sentinel, {W}, 1/1 Artifact Creature — Human Soldier, "whenever an opponent casts their first noncreature spell each turn, draw a card unless they pay X = this creature's power"; flavor: "The more Esper changes, the more he refuses to.") — first confirmed **Lathaar-tier** design. Personality explicitly corrected mid-design from plain "stubborn/immovable" to **grace and quiet omnipotence**: he doesn't react because nothing could actually threaten him, not because he's merely rigid. Mechanic reframed as a toll exacted from anyone attempting to enact change nearby. Confirmed design: broad-shouldered masculine build (explicit anchoring needed — default descriptive language drifted feminine without it, same failure as the Rakdos/Vaelgorath design); long silver-white hair; modest close-fitting gold circlet with small subtle horn-points and one white gem (an earlier oversized-horn version was scaled back); a geometric gold arm-pattern of straight lines/diamonds/chevrons (organic curling/vine versions were rejected three times running as reading "wood-elf" — geometric, not organic, is the tier's correct visual register); a layered high-collared ivory-and-gold robe (no scarf/beads — those are Vaelendris's own signature, not this tier's); an entirely gold ceremonial sword (crossed-blade-crest pommel, acanthus-engraved grip, foliate crossguard); one hand extended palm-up with a single gold coin suspended mid-fall above it (Rule 3 detail, tied directly to the toll mechanic); set on a natural cliffside overlook above an autumn valley with waterfalls (Rivendell-register nature, architecture minimal) at golden-hour light, with a deliberate cool blue-grey mist/sky patch preserved specifically to avoid an all-warm, contrast-less palette.
  - **Working note (design process):** Lathaar-tier's signature palette is **not yet a separate color rule** the way violet is locked for Maelgrith (Section 3) — this design's palette (warm ivory/gold with a cool mist/sky counterpoint) was arrived at by patching Vaelendris's own reference image's environment (swap dark robe for bright) and then correcting a full-autumn-gold drift back toward genuine warm/cool contrast, not by establishing an independent rule yet. Revisit if a second Lathaar-tier card's palette wants to diverge.

- **Drana and Linvala** (real card: {1}{W}{B}, Legendary Creature — Vampire Angel, 3/4, Flying/Vigilance, "opponents' creatures' activated abilities can't be activated; this creature has all activated abilities of all creatures your opponents control") — kept real names, built directly on the actual card art as reference rather than an invented scene. Not a single split-body character — a **paired duo**: Drana (Aelathir-tier corrupted vampire, left) and Linvala (Lathaar-tier unfallen angel, right), fighting side by side against a shared external violet-black void threat rather than against each other. Confirmed design: the two women separated by a clear gap of open air, not standing together — connected only by their two effects, each independently deflecting her own portion of the shared threat. Drana: pale cool-toned skin with red paint-streaks down from the eyes plus separate glowing pink-red cracks in the skin; two large asymmetric dark curved horns with jagged claw-like points (differing from each other, not mirrored); dark leather bodice with pointed pauldrons; torn skirt baring one leg hip-to-knee; a dagger with asymmetric serrated blade and a thorn-cluster pommel; a ribbon of pink-red energy erupting outward from her hand to repel (not absorb) the void tendril. Linvala: loose blonde hair, no hood; modest open-shoulder white gown; larger wingspan than Drana's; a long gold ceremonial staff (engraved shaft, sunburst-disc top, no blade/point) held overhead; a round gold-rimmed shield with a glowing blue-white face blocking her own separate void tendril.
  - **Working note (design process):** two real, reusable lessons here. First, when a scene shows two characters' powers meeting a third thing, the third thing (the void tendril here) needs its own clearly distinct color/substance stated explicitly, or the generator collapses it into whichever character's effect is already established — an early draft with no separately-colored void tendril read as Drana simply absorbing power into herself rather than repelling an outside threat. Second, when a character is meant to be *pushing an effect away from her body*, that directionality must be stated explicitly ("erupts outward from her hand, pushing away... repelling... back the direction it came from") — without it, the generator defaults to ambiguous or inward-reading energy flow. Both fixes are worth carrying into `art-template/SKILL.md` as general technique, not just this card's lore.

## 5. Open gaps

- **Mid-transformation tier (single body, visibly split half-and-half) still has no confirmed worked example.** Drana and Linvala was initially floated as a possible fit for this slot but turned out to be a paired-duo design (two separate characters) rather than one split body — the gap remains genuinely open.
- Lathaar-tier's own signature color/light rule (as opposed to a one-off palette patched from Vaelendris's reference) is still not formally locked — see Esper Sentinel's working note above.
- Avacyn, Angel of Hope and Avacyn, Angel of Horror — both confirmed done (per user, 2026-09-29); no design/flavor details recorded in this file since they were completed before this file existed.
